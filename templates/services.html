What changes:
• These rows leave the Material master and appear here instead.
• They stop appearing in Stock Summary, Dashboard and Dispatch reports — the negative balances above disappear.
• No GRN can be raised against them.
• A rent-only sale will post even with Allow Negative Stock off.
What does not change:
• Every bill, PDF, client ledger, payment and balance stays identical. Historical rows store the name as text, so they are not modified — they simply resolve here.
• Booked rent keeps working: booking, dispatch, cancellation and returns all read the same records.
Take a database backup first. The conversion is reversible (the material rows can be restored), but a snapshot is the standing convention before any structural change.
