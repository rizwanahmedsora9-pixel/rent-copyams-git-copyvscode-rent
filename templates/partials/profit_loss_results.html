{% endif %} {# --------------------------- READ THE RESULT ---------------------------- #}
Read the result in three steps
1. Sale is earned on delivery
Goods add revenue when they are dispatched, even if the customer pays later. A booking is an order; a receipt settles a bill. Neither creates profit a second time.
Delivered goods sales Rs. {{ money(bd.reconcile.goods_sales) }} · money received Rs. {{ money(payment_received_total) }}.
2. FIFO assigns the GRN cost
Each dispatch uses the oldest available GRN lot first. A GRN purchase is stock, not an immediate expense; its cost enters profit only when those goods are delivered.
Purchases in period Rs. {{ money(bd.grn_section.purchase_value if bd.grn_section else 0) }} · cost assigned to delivered goods Rs. {{ money(bd.reconcile.goods_cost) }}.
3. Net result is the remainder
Gross profit plus service margin plus other income, less operating expenses and other losses. "Total Loss" is the sum of loss rows; it is not the final net loss. Credit, advances and inventory value are positions and are never added in.
Net result for this period: Rs. {{ money(total_profit) }}.
