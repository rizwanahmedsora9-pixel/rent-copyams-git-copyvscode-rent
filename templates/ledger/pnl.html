How this is built
Every number above is a sum of posted journal lines for the period. Revenue is credited gross and discounts are debited to 4090, so the discount total is visible instead of being divided into the lines at render time. COGS comes from the frozen FIFO cost stored on each sale line (5010), and a line with no known cost posts nothing rather than an invented number — it is listed on the books-agree page.
